Privacy policy

Notes to PDF: Vector Export · Updated 19 November 2026

Publisher and contact

Balog Gergely Sándor. For privacy questions or requests, email alogergely@gmail.com.

Your notes and PDFs

The app processes the notes you select or share locally on your device. Its conversion code does not upload note content to the publisher or send it to the advertising SDK. Temporary PDFs can remain in the app cache; clear the cache in Android settings to remove them. PDFs saved to a location you choose remain there until you delete them. If you choose a cloud-backed document provider, that provider handles storage under its own terms. The app has no user account.

Audience and age choices

This app is intended for high-school and university students. Before advertising starts, you can select an age band or skip ads. The app stores only that band locally in app preferences, not a date of birth. The selection is used to configure age-appropriate advertising, and the corresponding restricted-treatment signals are sent to Google when advertising is allowed. All under-18 users receive the stricter restricted-ad treatment and general-audience ad content; no rewarded ads are requested for under-13 users or when the age selection is skipped. Free image export remains available. Use “Age group & ad settings” to change the selection or skip ads. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the stored age choice.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob is the only integrated advertising service. The app also integrates Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy choices. The advertising SDK may collect IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device/account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This is separate from note conversion. Google documents encrypted transmission for SDK data. The options available depend on your location, age group and the publisher’s AdMob configuration. Where consent is required for personalized advertising or device storage, the publisher relies on the choice you make in the consent form. The European consent form identifies advertising partners and explains their purposes and legal bases. In covered US states, the applicable privacy message can offer the choice to opt out of certain uses of your data. Where available, use “Ad privacy choices” in the app to revisit your choices. Free image PDF export remains available offline. Ad loading may occur before you choose a rewarded export, when the consent gate permits it.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. Google's processing, retention periods and international transfer safeguards are described in its policies; the publisher cannot erase Google's records simply by deleting the app.

Support correspondence and retention

When you write to alogergely@gmail.com, the publisher uses your email address, message and any attachments to answer your question or investigate the issue you reported. Google provides the Gmail mailbox. You can describe most problems without attaching a private note. Support emails and their attachments are deleted one month after the support case is closed. The publisher counts the month from closure and deletes the correspondence from the support mailbox, including sent replies. For ordinary support requests, the publisher relies on the legitimate interest in responding to users and resolving problems under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR where it applies. Requests to exercise a statutory privacy right are handled to meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can delete exported documents, clear local app data, uninstall the app and use Android's advertising-ID controls. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify or erase personal data, restrict or object to processing, request portability and withdraw consent. Contact the publisher for requests relating to data the publisher holds; use Google's privacy controls for data Google handles. You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including Hungary's NAIH.
